Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · Applies to: Faceless Channel Automation

This privacy policy explains what data Faceless Channel Automation ("the app") accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected. The app is a personal automation tool built and operated solely by me, Andre Al Thubian, to run my own YouTube channel. It is not distributed to, or used by, any other person.

Who operates this app

Andre Al Thubian (sole operator and sole user). Contact: andre.althubian@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

  • Google Sheets data: the app reads and writes to a single Google Sheet that I own, which contains video topics, generated scripts, render status, and links to published videos. This sheet is not shared with or accessible by anyone besides me.
  • YouTube channel data: the app uses the YouTube Data API, authenticated as my own Google account, to upload finished videos, set video titles/descriptions/tags, and set video thumbnails on my own YouTube channel. It does not read, modify, or access any other YouTube channel, and it does not access viewer, subscriber, or analytics data beyond what is needed to confirm an upload succeeded.

What data the app does not collect

The app does not collect data from, or about, any third party. It does not have end users other than me, does not serve a public sign-up flow, does not collect visitor analytics on this website, and does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.

How the data is used

Data read from the Google Sheet (topics and scripts) is sent to third-party services I use to generate scripts, synthesize voiceover, source stock footage, and render video files, purely as part of producing a finished video. The resulting video file is then uploaded to my own YouTube channel. Status information (render status, the resulting YouTube video link, timestamps) is written back to the same Google Sheet so I can track what has been published. No data collected or generated by this process is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or any other third party for any purpose unrelated to producing and publishing these videos.

Data retention

Sheet data and rendered video metadata are retained indefinitely in my own Google Sheet and YouTube account for my own record-keeping, and can be deleted by me at any time. Intermediate files used during video production (such as temporary render files) are not retained longer than needed to complete the upload.

Third-party services involved in production

Producing each video involves passing script text and related metadata to third-party APIs for AI text generation, text-to-speech, stock footage search, and video rendering, strictly to produce the video. Each of these services is used under its own terms and privacy policy, and none of them are provided with any personal data beyond what's required to generate that specific video's content.

Google API Services User Data Policy

This app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If what this app accesses or how it uses that access changes, this page will be updated accordingly, and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to andre.althubian@gmail.com.
